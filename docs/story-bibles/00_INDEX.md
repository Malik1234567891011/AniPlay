# Plotbreak — New Official Story Concepts

This folder contains four fully developed official-world bibles designed around the product lessons learned from OOC's creator flow and Plotbreak's own recent gameplay audits.

## Stories

1. **HUSH HOUSE** — supernatural horror / romance. A beautiful neighbor warns you not to open the door when “she” knocks at 2:13 a.m.
2. **WINDOW SEVEN** — stakeout espionage thriller. Seven nights watching a suspected traitor with an elite undercover partner.
3. **GOOD MORNING, HUSBAND** — feel-good domestic romance. You wake into a loving marriage you do not remember.
4. **PRIMAL CROWN** — prehistoric faction adventure. Raptors, sabertooths, mammoths, horned giants, sky riders, migration politics.

## Shared authoring philosophy

- Author the world deeply; do not author the player's route deeply.
- Give every major character clear wants, fears, secrets, voice, and visual identity.
- Keep structured state small and story-serving.
- Use retrievable lore / keyword-book style context rather than dumping all world detail into every turn.
- Use reaction-image libraries for fast ordinary character beats; use bespoke hero frames for major events.
- Let images appear at cool moments; do not force one every turn.
- Let prose breathe. OOC demonstrates that long story text can work when it is readable, specific, and responsive.
- Endings are possible destinations / eligibility conditions, not rails.
- Player can break intended arcs; the world adapts.
- Main-screen gameplay should remain simple: read, see, type anything, story reacts.
