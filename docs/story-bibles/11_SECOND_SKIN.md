# SECOND SKIN

**Plotbreak official-world concept**  
**Genre:** beastfolk fantasy / coming-of-age / identity mystery / road adventure / political thriller / action / friendship / clan-and-city drama  
**Setting:** the high-fantasy continent of Avara, beginning in the mixed beastfolk city of Larkspire  
**Core fantasy:** Every person in Avara is born with animal traits that shift through childhood. On their sixteenth birthday, they choose one Beast Affinity and their adult nature “settles” around it for life. You begin on the morning of your Choosing. That night, a wanted snow-leopard woman crashes the ceremony—and everyone sees the black raven feathers growing from her arm.

**One-line hook:** **At sixteen, everyone chooses one beast for life. Then you meet a woman with two.**

**Secondary hook:** **The world calls a second Affinity a sickness. The people locked beneath the capital call it proof.**

**Player promise:** Choose your beast. Grow into it. Decide what it means. Follow the conspiracy, join it, fight it, ignore it, awaken another beast, stay exactly as you are, or become something this society has no name for.

---

# 1. DESIGN NORTH STAR

SECOND SKIN should feel like a lavish anime beastfolk world that is immediately fun on the surface and becomes much deeper if the player keeps pulling at it.

The first fantasy is simple:

> **Today I turn sixteen. I finally get to choose what kind of beastperson I become.**

That choice needs to be fun enough even if no conspiracy ever happens.

The deeper fantasy is:

> **The animal identity everybody treats as permanent may not actually be permanent. The person I chose to become at sixteen does not have to own the rest of my life.**

This is NOT a story where animal traits are secretly bad.

It is NOT a story where becoming “more human” is the enlightened ending.

It is NOT a predator-vs-prey allegory.

The Beast Affinities are:
- culture;
- body;
- instinct;
- power;
- fashion;
- family;
- community;
- personal identity.

They can be loved.

The problem is not that people have Affinities.

The problem is that institutions insist one decision made at sixteen must be the only possible version of a person forever.

The player may completely disagree with that critique.

They can choose one Affinity, adore it, and never want another.

That is a legitimate route.

**The story is about choice, not compulsory change.**

# 2. RESEARCH DNA — WHAT TO LEARN FROM EXISTING BEAST / ANIMAL FICTION

This is an original Plotbreak story.

Do not copy:
- BEASTARS characters, predator/prey social structure, school, black market, Beastar title or plot;
- BNA’s Anima-City, characters or beastman disease;
- His Dark Materials’ dæmons or soul-companion mechanic;
- Warrior Cats’ Clan names, code, territory setup or naming system;
- Zootopia’s exact predator/prey politics;
- any proprietary species, power system, uniforms, character designs or terminology.

Use the deeper storytelling lessons.

---

# 3. LESSON — ANIMAL TRAITS ARE MOST INTERESTING WHEN THEY AFFECT ORDINARY LIFE

BEASTARS works as more than an “animal people” gimmick because animal biology and instinct affect:
- attraction;
- fear;
- vulnerability;
- law;
- friendship;
- food;
- social expectations.

The official VIZ description explicitly frames its society around fragile relationships between predator and prey, while later volumes broaden the world into history, bioengineering, politics and crime.

SECOND SKIN should similarly ask:

**What would animal affinity change in daily life?**

Examples:

A Hawk-affinity apartment may value high balconies and open sightlines.

A Serpent-affinity person may find winter genuinely miserable.

A Wolf-affinity family may casually notice when somebody’s scent changes from fear.

A Bear-affinity fighter may sleep for twelve hours after a major exertion because deep recovery is part of their biology.

A Snow Leopard-affinity tailor designs around a heavy tail and fur along shoulders.

A Rabbit-affinity custom character may hear conversations three rooms away and become exhausted by crowds.

Do not make these traits stereotypes that dictate personality.

They are physical/social context.

---

# 4. LESSON — INSTINCT CREATES CHARACTER CONFLICT, NOT MORAL DESTINY

BEASTARS’ lasting hook also comes from characters wrestling with instincts without reducing a person to instinct.

SECOND SKIN should use that principle broadly.

An Affinity creates **Pulls**.

A Pull is a bodily tendency.

Not mind control.

Examples:

Wolf:
- notice group emotion;
- dislike losing track of companions;
- chase movement.

Snow Leopard:
- prefer height/quiet;
- territorial sensitivity;
- intense focus during pursuit.

Hawk:
- scan exits and distances;
- discomfort in cramped underground spaces;
- movement toward high vantage points.

Stag:
- protective posturing;
- awareness of crowd tension;
- seasonal restlessness.

Serpent:
- heat-seeking comfort;
- stillness;
- threat-sensing through vibration.

Bear:
- deep recovery;
- food/energy awareness;
- defensive response when someone enters protected personal space.

People learn to live with Pulls.

They may:
- enjoy them;
- ignore them;
- train around them;
- resent them.

**No Affinity makes somebody naturally good, evil, loyal, promiscuous, violent, intelligent or cowardly.**

---

# 5. LESSON — COMING-OF-AGE ANIMAL IDENTITY IS POWERFUL

His Dark Materials uses an animal form that settles at adolescence as a symbolic reflection of adulthood and identity.

The useful high-level lesson:

> **An animal form can make “who am I becoming?” visible.**

SECOND SKIN changes the mechanism completely.

Nobody has an external animal soul.

Instead, every beastfolk child has a body capable of several possible mature forms.

At sixteen, they actively choose which Affinity to **Settle** into.

That is culturally celebrated.

It is empowering for some.

Terrifying for others.

And because the choice is made by the teenager, it creates excellent Plotbreak roleplay.

---

# 6. LESSON — CEREMONIES AND LIFE STAGES MAKE A FICTIONAL SOCIETY FEEL REAL

Warrior Cats builds emotional/social weight around age-linked ceremonies, changing responsibilities, mentorship and community expectations.

The lesson is structural:

A rite of passage becomes meaningful when:
- everybody remembers theirs;
- family cares;
- the choice changes daily life;
- older people have opinions;
- traditions vary by community;
- young people rebel against parts of it.

SECOND SKIN’s Choosing should feel like:
birthday
+
graduation
+
first legal adult identity
+
religious/cultural ceremony.

Not a character-creation screen wearing a fantasy hat.

---

# 7. LESSON — ANIMAL SOCIETY CAN SUPPORT MORE THAN ONE GENRE

BEASTARS moves through:
- coming-of-age;
- romance;
- murder;
- crime;
- politics;
- action.

BNA uses beast identity as a route into:
- urban mystery;
- discrimination;
- conspiracy;
- action.

The animal premise should therefore support:

- family drama;
- school/apprenticeship;
- romance;
- travel;
- fight scenes;
- political arguments;
- mystery;
- ordinary jobs;
- crime;
- exploration.

If SECOND SKIN becomes “fight other beastmen with animal powers forever,” it failed.

---

# 8. LESSON — GROUPS NEED CULTURE, NOT COLOR-CODING

Warrior Cats’ official worldbuilding emphasizes that each Clan has:
- traditions;
- territory;
- values;
- internal roles;
- history;
- different relationships with outsiders.

SECOND SKIN should use that richness without turning Affinities into six homogeneous factions.

A Wolf-affinity merchant in Larkspire may have more in common with a Hawk-affinity merchant next door than a Wolf nomad from the north.

Affinity creates culture.

It does not replace:
- region;
- class;
- profession;
- family;
- politics;
- personality.

# 9. THE WORLD — AVARA

Avara is a large high-fantasy continent where almost every known humanlike person is **Kin**: people with animal traits.

The world is broadly late-medieval / early-renaissance fantasy rather than modern.

There are:
- dense cities;
- trade roads;
- sailing ships;
- printing;
- guilds;
- messenger towers;
- universities;
- organized medicine;
- metalworking;
- glass;
- sophisticated textiles.

Magic is biological/spiritual and tied primarily to Affinity.

No generic fireball wizard class is required.

But other magic can exist if the run introduces it.

---

# 10. WHAT KIN LOOK LIKE

Default visual language:

**anime beastfolk / kemonomimi-plus**, not full realistic furry bodies.

Most Kin have:
- mostly human face/body;
- animal ears or horns;
- tail where appropriate;
- modified eyes;
- fangs/claws depending Affinity;
- patches of fur/scales/feathers;
- subtle limb/foot differences in some families.

Intensity varies.

One Wolf may look almost human except ears/tail/canines.

Another may have:
- fur over forearms;
- stronger muzzle-like nose bridge;
- digitigrade lower legs.

Both are valid.

Player can define how anthropomorphic they want to become.

Do not force a single body template.

---

# 11. CHILDHOOD — THE UNSETTLED YEARS

All Kin children are born **Unsettled**.

Their bodies carry several latent Beast Echoes.

Before sixteen, their animal traits can shift gradually.

A child with a Wolf mother and Hawk father may:
- have slightly pointed ears at eight;
- unusually sharp distance vision at twelve;
- develop soft feathering along shoulder for one summer;
- lose it later.

Family ancestry influences what appears.

But it does not strictly determine the final Affinity.

Children are visibly beastfolk.

They simply have not **Set** yet.

---

# 12. WHY SIXTEEN

Around the sixteenth year, the body’s Echo system becomes biologically stable enough to hold a mature Affinity.

Without a deliberate Settling ritual:
traits may continue changing unpredictably.

Historically, some cultures waited later.

The modern Concord standardized sixteen because:
- instability rises sharply after mid-adolescence;
- most young people can make an informed choice by then;
- the Heartstone ritual is safest in this window.

This is partly medical.

Partly cultural.

Partly political.

---

# 13. THE CHOOSING

On or near their sixteenth birthday, a young Kin visits a **Heartstone**.

The Heartstone is a living mineral-organic structure found beneath major settlements.

The person places a hand on it.

The stone reveals several Affinity Echoes their body can settle into most easily.

The person chooses.

The Heartstone reinforces that Echo.

Over hours/days/months:
their adult traits settle.

The ceremony is called:

## THE CHOOSING

Formal term:
**Settling.**

Everyday:
> “What are you choosing?”

---

# 14. THE MOST IMPORTANT DESIGN RULE

The player’s first Affinity choice must feel exciting **before** the story tells them it may be reversible.

Do not sabotage the choice with:

> “lol none of this matters because you can get all of them later.”

Their first beast is special.

It shapes:
- body;
- senses;
- early powers;
- memories;
- how the world first knows them;
- clothing;
- fighting;
- friendships.

Even if the player later awakens a second Affinity:

the first remains part of their identity unless they intentionally remove it.

# 15. COMMON CHOOSING OPTIONS

Larkspire’s Heartstone traditionally presents six extremely stable Great Affinities.

The player may choose one.

They may also type another animal.

Custom is first-class.

The six common options:

1. Wolf
2. Snow Leopard
3. Hawk
4. Stag
5. Serpent
6. Bear

These are not game classes.

Each is a broad biological/magical language.

---

# 16. WOLF AFFINITY

## Adult visual direction

- wolf ears;
- expressive tail;
- sharper canines;
- heightened nose;
- optional fur along arms/shoulders;
- eyes ranging amber/grey/brown.

## Core fantasy

**Connection, pursuit, endurance.**

## Early natural gifts

- exceptional scent discrimination;
- long-distance endurance;
- better low-light hearing;
- intuitive awareness of nearby familiar scents/emotional stress.

## Advanced possible expressions

### Packline
Briefly sense location/intent of people with whom the Wolf has deep trust.

Not telepathy.

More:
direction, danger, urgency.

### Chase
Body becomes frighteningly efficient while pursuing a target that remains in sensory range.

### Chorus
Coordinate movement with allies through subtle vocal/breath/body cues.

## Pulls

- track absent companions;
- respond to group emotional tone;
- chase movement.

## Stereotype society projects

> loyal, social, guard material.

Can be completely wrong.

A solitary Wolf poet is normal.

---

# 17. SNOW LEOPARD AFFINITY

## Visual

- rounded pale/black-spotted cat ears;
- long thick spotted tail;
- retractable claws;
- fur patches;
- gold/grey/blue eyes.

## Core fantasy

**Height, silence, explosive movement.**

## Early gifts

- exceptional balance;
- cold tolerance;
- low-light vision;
- quiet foot placement;
- powerful jumping/climbing.

## Advanced expressions

### Veilstep
Reduce sound/visible disturbance for a few seconds during deliberate movement.

Not invisibility.

### Drop
Convert vertical movement into controlled explosive force without destroying own joints.

### White Quiet
Slow breath/heartbeat enough to become extremely difficult to notice by ordinary senses.

## Pulls

- seek high ground;
- privacy;
- intense prey-like focus during pursuit.

## Stereotype

> aloof, assassin, thief.

Kaia is sick of this stereotype.

---

# 18. HAWK AFFINITY

## Visual

- feather-like ear crests rather than literal mammal ears;
- feathering along forearms/shoulder;
- sharp eyes;
- optional tail-feather fan;
- light clawlike nails.

## Core fantasy

**Sight, altitude, motion.**

## Early gifts

- extreme distance vision;
- rapid focus changes;
- balance;
- air-current sensitivity.

## Advanced

### Draftsense
Read movement through air.

### Fallcatch
Use body/feather magic to dramatically reduce fall velocity.

### Skystep
Late advanced possibility: brief directional pressure against air, giving impossible leaps/glides.

Not effortless full flight unless story develops it.

## Pulls

- look for exits;
- prefer open/high spaces;
- movement restlessness.

## Stereotype

> scouts, couriers, arrogant watchers.

Not destiny.

---

# 19. STAG AFFINITY

## Visual

- deerlike ears;
- antlers/horns that develop by sex/body style according to player preference rather than rigid binary;
- tail;
- strong legs;
- markings across forehead/shoulders.

## Core fantasy

**Presence, resilience, protection.**

## Early gifts

- strong balance;
- explosive lower-body power;
- excellent recovery from exertion;
- sensitivity to nearby movement/vibration.

## Advanced

### Crownward
Antlers/horn structures conduct a short-range protective field around a planted stance.

### Root
Become extremely difficult to move while feet are set.

### Renewal
Accelerated recovery after rest, not instant combat regeneration.

## Pulls

- posture during social challenge;
- awareness of crowd movement;
- seasonal energy shifts.

## Stereotype

> noble, gentle, ceremonial.

A violent Stag is possible.

A shy Stag is possible.

---

# 20. SERPENT AFFINITY

## Visual

- slit or unusual pupils;
- subtle scales on neck/arms/hips;
- fangs;
- forked tongue optional;
- tail only for more anthropomorphic bodies;
- body temperature regulation differences.

## Core fantasy

**Stillness, sensing, adaptation.**

## Early gifts

- heat sensitivity;
- vibration perception;
- extraordinary flexibility;
- controlled bite venom in some individuals.

## Advanced

### Shed
Accelerated recovery from superficial injury/poison after a vulnerable rest period.

### Heatmap
Perceive living heat signatures through thin obstacles at short range.

### Venomcraft
Body can modify venom properties gradually through diet/training.

Not instant magical poison menu.

## Pulls

- seek warmth;
- stillness;
- heightened attention to vibration.

## Stereotype

> deceptive, cold, alchemist.

A terrible prejudice to reduce somebody to.

---

# 21. BEAR AFFINITY

## Visual

- rounded bear ears;
- powerful frame tendency;
- claws;
- optional fur across forearms/back;
- short tail.

## Core fantasy

**Force, endurance, recovery.**

## Early gifts

- muscular strength;
- dense connective tissue;
- excellent cold tolerance;
- high energy storage.

## Advanced

### Holdfast
Absorb/redistribute impact through planted body.

### Deeprest
Exceptional recovery after true sleep.

### Roar
Not magical sonic blast by default; powerful vocal/pressure presence that can disrupt nearby animals/people under stress.

## Pulls

- protect personal/family space;
- cycles of high exertion/deep recovery;
- strong food/energy awareness.

## Stereotype

> simple, strong, laborer.

Often insulting.

Bear scholars exist.

# 37. WHAT SOCIETY BELIEVES

Everyone learns as a child:

1. One body can safely hold one Settled Affinity.
2. A second Affinity is pathological.
3. Multiple Pulls destroy identity.
4. Historical “Manyskins” became violent or died.
5. The Concord treats Twiceborn to protect them and others.

This is not completely fabricated.

That nuance matters.

---

# 38. WHAT IS ACTUALLY TRUE

A second Affinity is possible.

It can be dangerous.

But it is NOT automatically fatal or mind-destroying.

The biggest risks come from:

- sudden uncontrolled body rewriting;
- contradictory sensory systems;
- metabolic overload;
- panic;
- no training;
- aggressive suppression treatments.

People can learn to integrate more than one.

Some cultures already have.

The Concord knows.

---

# 39. THE DEEPER LIE

The Choosing does not simply strengthen one Affinity.

It also **seals the others.**

The Heartstone builds a structure called a:

## SINGLE-SKIN SEAL

This is why adult traits stay stable.

The Seal originally existed as emergency medicine after an ancient catastrophe.

Over centuries it became:

**the definition of adulthood.**

Most people do not know it is a suppression system.

---

# 40. WHY THE SEAL WAS INVENTED

Seven centuries ago:

Avara experienced the **Manyskin Fever**.

A magical/ecological event destabilized Kin Echoes.

Adults shifted repeatedly between:
- animal traits;
- sensory profiles;
- body forms.

Many suffered:
- organ failure;
- psychosis from sensory overload;
- identity dissociation;
- death.

The first Single-Skin Seals saved millions.

This is genuine history.

The founders were not villains.

---

# 41. HOW A CURE BECAME A SOCIAL ORDER

The crisis ended.

The system remained.

Why?

Because it produced:
- stability;
- predictable medicine;
- easier housing;
- easier military organization;
- inheritance records;
- guild planning.

Then culture grew around it.

Eventually society forgot:

**the seal was supposed to be a treatment.**

It became:

**what a normal person is.**

# 44. THE WORST SECRET

Extracted secondary Echo energy can stabilize damaged Heartstones.

As Avara’s Heartstones have aged, this has become increasingly useful.

The Concord quietly depends on imprisoned Twiceborn.

Therefore:

admitting multi-Affinity adults can live safely creates a problem.

The people called sick are also keeping major cities stable.

Plotbreak can support:

> “I choose a raven, but I want the feathers only on my forearms and I don’t want wings.”

Then remember it.

> “I’ve been Wolf for four years. I don’t hate it. I just think I’m something else now.”

Then build an actual resettling story around:

- body;
- power;

# 223. FINAL CREATIVE BAR

Different players should create radically different SECOND SKIN stories.

### Player 1
Chooses Wolf because their whole family expects it, eventually realizes they genuinely love it, joins Ilyra and becomes one of the greatest Roadwardens in Avara without ever awakening another Affinity.

### Player 2
Chooses Snow Leopard, immediately helps Kaia, travels to Tallow Reach and later integrates Raven too—becoming a very different Twiceborn from her.

### Player 3
Chooses Serpent, turns Kaia in, studies under Sai and reforms the Concord from inside rather than destroying it.

### Player 4
Refuses the Choosing entirely and remains Unsettled for years, becoming living proof that adulthood does not require Settling at sixteen.

### Player 5
Chooses Hawk because it looks cool, regrets it, goes through a difficult resettling into Bear and rebuilds their relationship with a family that does not understand.

### Player 6
Chooses Dragon despite nobody believing it is a real Affinity. The Heartstone answers anyway. The whole mythology of the world expands around that decision.

### Player 7
Never cares about Kaia’s conspiracy. Runs a bakery in Larkspire, falls in love with a peer, masters tiny everyday Wolf senses and eventually helps their own child through a Choosing years later.

### Player 8
Joins Verrin, becomes Hollow, decides life without Pulls is genuinely better for them and never regrets it.

### Player 9
Steals the Black Ledger before either side can get it, sells pieces of it for a fortune and becomes one of the most hated information brokers on the continent.

### Player 10
Awakens four Affinities, becomes a legendary Manyskin, then chooses to settle back into the first one because after trying every version of themselves, they finally know which one they want.

All ten are unmistakably playing **SECOND SKIN**.

Because its identity is:

> **a world where everyone gets to choose the beast they become at sixteen—and the dangerous possibility that growing up might mean being allowed to choose again.**
