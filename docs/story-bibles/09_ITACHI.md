# ITACHI

**Plotbreak official-world / licensed-IP-style fan scenario bible**  
**Source character:** Itachi Uchiha from *Naruto*  
**Genre:** shinobi tragedy / political thriller / coming-of-age / espionage / family drama / war / Akatsuki infiltration / psychological action  
**Default span:** age 4 through death and reanimation  
**Core fantasy:** You are Itachi Uchiha before history has decided what kind of man he becomes. You are a prodigy born into a clan the village fears, a village your clan no longer trusts, and a family that expects you to somehow belong completely to both. You can follow the known path, resist it, expose it, save people who originally died, make worse choices, become Hokage, join the coup, flee with Sasuke, kill Danzō, refuse the massacre, or still become the Itachi everyone remembers for entirely different reasons.

**Title:** **ITACHI**

**One-line hook:** **Before he became a traitor, he was a child asked to choose between his family and his village.**

**Secondary hook:** **Live Itachi Uchiha’s life from the Third Shinobi World War to the Akatsuki—and decide which sacrifices still happen when the choices are yours.**

---

# 1. CREATIVE NORTH STAR

This world should not feel like:

> “Naruto plot summary with buttons.”

It should feel like:

> **I am actually living inside Itachi’s life, with enough time between the famous moments to understand why each decision became possible.**

The player is **Itachi Uchiha**.

Not an original ninja standing beside him.

That changes how this story must be authored.

The player inherits:
- Itachi’s family;
- his extraordinary talent;
- the Uchiha clan;
- the Sharingan;
- Konoha;
- the historical pressures around the coup;
- Sasuke;
- Shisui;
- the ANBU;
- Danzō;
- Akatsuki;
- the known political structure.

But the player does **not** inherit one mandatory decision.

Plotbreak’s entire advantage is:

> **Canon creates pressure. The player creates history.**

The world should therefore preserve two things simultaneously:

1. **The emotional truth of Itachi’s life.**
2. **The freedom to refuse Itachi’s canon choices.**

The user should be able to understand why canon Itachi made the choices he made without being railroaded into repeating them.

---

# 2. RESEARCH SOURCE HIERARCHY

There are multiple versions of Itachi’s past.

For implementation, use a deliberate hierarchy instead of accidentally mixing contradictions.

## Tier 1 — Manga core canon

Masashi Kishimoto’s manga is the primary backbone.

It establishes the indispensable life structure:
- Itachi sees war extremely young;
- graduates Academy very young;
- awakens Sharingan;
- rises rapidly;
- clan coup;
- double-agent position;
- Shisui;
- Uchiha massacre;
- Sasuke spared;
- Akatsuki;
- illness;
- fight/death against Sasuke;
- truth revealed;
- reanimation;
- Kotoamatsukami freeing him;
- Kabuto/Izanami;
- final reconciliation with Sasuke.

If later material contradicts the manga:
manga core wins.

---

## Tier 2 — *Itachi Shinden: Book of Bright Light* and *Book of Dark Night*

Use these novels to deepen:
- childhood;
- Academy;
- Genin years;
- Izumi;
- Tenma/Shinko;
- Shisui friendship;
- Mukai Kohinata mission;
- ANBU entry;
- coup detail;
- Danzō manipulation;
- massacre psychology.

Where novel and anime adaptation conflict:
prefer the novel by default because it is the prose source the anime arc loosely adapts.

---

## Tier 3 — Anime-expanded Itachi story

Use non-contradictory material from the *Itachi’s Story: Light and Darkness* anime arc when it improves playable life:
- visualized childhood moments;
- Team dynamics;
- ANBU/Foundation conflicts;
- Jūzō partnership;
- extra Akatsuki missions.

Treat clearly anime-specific sequences as **expanded continuity**, not mandatory manga events.

---

## Tier 4 — Databook / official retrospective details

Useful for:
- ages;
- rank timing;
- favorite foods;
- broad characterization;
- chronology reconciliation.

Do not force dubious stat-sheet minutiae into prose.

# 3. IMPORTANT TIMELINE NOTE

Itachi’s chronology has historically contained age inconsistencies across older material.

The most coherent composite for this story:

- **Age 4:** witnesses Third Shinobi World War battlefield aftermath.
- **Age 5:** Sasuke is born; Nine-Tails attack occurs around this early-childhood period.
- **Age 6:** Academy entry.
- **Age 7:** Academy graduation.
- **Age 8:** Sharingan awakens after teammate trauma/death in expanded Shinden continuity.
- **Age 10:** promoted to Chūnin.
- **Age 11:** ANBU entry period in Shinden chronology.
- **Age 12:** ANBU captain promotion; official records can list thirteen, explaining older statements.
- **Around 13:** coup crisis / Shisui death / Uchiha massacre.
- **Teen years:** Akatsuki.
- **Age 17–18 range:** Part I return to Konoha.
- **Age 21:** final battle with Sasuke / death.
- **After death:** reanimated during Fourth Shinobi World War.

Do not constantly state ages in-story.

Use them to keep relationships and maturity coherent.

---

# 4. WHY ITACHI IS SUCH A STRONG PLAYABLE CHARACTER

The interesting thing about Itachi is not that he is powerful.

It is that his strongest traits repeatedly become the source of his worst decisions.

He is:
- brilliant;
- observant;
- self-sacrificing;
- disciplined;
- politically aware;
- capable of carrying pain alone.

Those qualities make him exceptional.

They also make it possible for a twelve/thirteen-year-old to believe:

> **Maybe I really am the person who should carry an entire political catastrophe by myself.**

His tragedy is partly that adults repeatedly allow a gifted child to act like an adult because he is useful.

The story should understand this.

Danzō sees:
a weapon mature enough to make impossible decisions.

Hiruzen sees:
an exceptional shinobi who may mediate.

Fugaku sees:
a son capable of restoring Uchiha power.

The clan sees:
its prodigy.

Sasuke sees:
his brother.

Shisui sees:
his closest friend.

Itachi tries to be all of these at once.

That is the pressure cooker.

---

# 5. THE CENTRAL THEMATIC QUESTION

The Itachi story is often summarized as sacrifice for peace.

For Plotbreak, the deeper interactive question should be:

> **When does sacrifice stop being noble and become another way of controlling other people’s choices?**

Canon Itachi decides for:
- the clan;
- Sasuke;
- the village;
- himself.

He believes lying to Sasuke will protect him.

He believes becoming hated is preferable to sharing the burden.

By the time he is reanimated, he has learned something important:

> trying to manipulate Sasuke’s path from the shadows was a mistake.

This gives the whole-life structure a beautiful arc:

### Young Itachi
“I can understand the whole board.”

### ANBU Itachi
“I can carry the whole board.”

### Akatsuki Itachi
“I can arrange the board so Sasuke becomes what he needs to become.”

### Reanimated Itachi
“I should have trusted people enough to let them choose.”

The player can learn that earlier.

Or never learn it.

---

# 6. DEFAULT OPENING — AGE FOUR

The story begins before the Academy.

Itachi is four.

Fugaku has taken him near the aftermath of the Third Shinobi World War.

Do not open with a giant lore speech.

Open concrete.

Mud.

Blood.

Destroyed terrain.

A dying shinobi.

Adults who have stopped reacting to death because there is too much of it.

Itachi is small enough that the bodies look enormous.

This is the first moment the player should understand:

**the boy is thinking about war much earlier than any child should.**

---

# 7. OPENING SCENE

Fugaku believes Itachi needs to understand the reality of being a shinobi.

The player sees:
- wounded enemy;
- wounded Leaf ninja;
- medics overwhelmed;
- a survivor begging for water;
- soldiers debating whether to move.

The exact first choice should not be:

A. Hate war  
B. Love war

Instead, let the player act.

Possible responses:

### Help
> I kneel beside the closest wounded man even though I do not know which village he is from. “Can I give him water?”

### Ask Fugaku
> I keep looking at the bodies. “If every village says it’s protecting its people, why are all their people here?”

### Shut down
> I say nothing. I stare until Father tells me it is time to move.

### Hardening
> “Then I need to become strong enough that nobody can bring this to our home.”

Each plants a different interpretation of strength.

---

# 8. THE FIRST INTERNAL MEMORY

Store an **early war belief**.

Possible semantic states:
- peace through empathy;
- peace through power;
- peace through control;
- disgust with shinobi system;
- emotional detachment;
- unresolved.

Do not show a meter.

Later:
Shisui, Fugaku, Danzō and Hiruzen can all interact differently with what the player actually believes.

---

# 9. FAMILY — FUGAKU UCHIHA

**Role:** father / clan leader / Konoha Military Police leader / political pressure / trainer  
**Relationship to player:** proud, emotionally restrained, increasingly conflicted

Fugaku must not be flattened into:
“bad dad who wants coup.”

He loves his sons.

He also sees Itachi as:
- extraordinary;
- clan asset;
- future leader;
- bridge to village.

That is unfair to a child even when motivated by love.

---

# 10. FUGAKU’S CORE BELIEF

> **A clan that accepts humiliation indefinitely teaches the village that humiliating it is safe.**

He does not begin wanting mass bloodshed.

He believes the Uchiha are:
- being isolated;
- suspected;
- watched;
- denied meaningful political power.

The Nine-Tails attack intensifies this.

His anger has real foundations.

His proposed solution becomes increasingly dangerous.

---

# 11. FUGAKU’S PRESSURE ON ITACHI

Fugaku trains Itachi hard.

Praises achievement.

Uses him politically.

But he also:
- watches Sasuke with pride;
- can soften privately;
- sometimes tells Itachi to choose for himself in Shinden material.

The relationship is contradictory.

That is good.

The player may:
- adore him;
- resent him;
- obey;
- confront;
- kill;
- save.

---

# 12. MIKOTO UCHIHA

**Role:** mother / emotional center / clan loyalty / Sasuke’s protector

Mikoto is often underused.

Plotbreak should make her real.

She:
- knows both sons;
- recognizes Itachi is carrying too much;
- supports her husband and clan broadly;
- does not need to agree with every radical step.

She should have:
- private conversations;
- meals;
- quiet worry;
- reactions to Itachi distancing.

Do not make her only “mother who smiles before massacre.”

---

# 13. MIKOTO’S VIEW

Possible default:

Mikoto believes the Uchiha deserve dignity.

She fears war.

She hopes Fugaku still has control of the clan.

She sees Itachi becoming emotionally unreachable and does not know whether pushing harder will make him disappear faster.

This gives the player someone to tell.

Canon Itachi does not tell enough.

Plotbreak can.

---

# 14. SASUKE UCHIHA

**Role:** younger brother / deepest emotional bond / moral pressure / future consequence of nearly every major decision

At opening:
infant/toddler later.

As childhood progresses:
Sasuke becomes:
- playful;
- admiring;
- competitive;
- desperate for Itachi’s attention.

Do not write Sasuke as revenge machine before trauma.

Young Sasuke is a kid who loves his brother.

That is why the later story hurts.

---

# 15. SASUKE’S CHILDHOOD NEED

> **See me.**

He sees Itachi receive:
- praise;
- missions;
- father’s political attention.

He wants:
- training;
- acknowledgment;
- time.

Canon Itachi’s forehead-poke habit should exist as affectionate distancing, but do not spam it as fanservice.

If player chooses to spend more time with Sasuke:
relationship genuinely changes.

---

# 16. PLAYER CAN TELL SASUKE THE TRUTH EARLY

This is one of the biggest branch points.

Possible ages:
- child;
- Academy;
- pre-coup.

If player says:

> “The clan and village are fighting politically. Father wants me involved.”

Sasuke may not understand fully.

But he is no longer completely blind.

This can drastically alter later trauma.

Do not force canonical ignorance.

---

# 17. SHISUI UCHIHA

**Role:** best friend / older-brother figure / ideological partner / emotional equal  
**Importance:** arguably the most important relationship besides Sasuke in Itachi’s formative years

Shisui should appear early.

Not only five minutes before death.

The player needs to:
- train with him;
- joke;
- argue;
- travel;
- experience trust.

Otherwise his death is plot information, not grief.

---

# 18. SHISUI’S PERSONALITY

Skilled.

Warm.

More socially comfortable than Itachi.

Able to challenge him.

He understands:
- clan pain;
- village loyalty.

He is not blind loyalist to leadership.

His ideal is:
protect peace without chasing personal glory.

---

# 19. SHISUI’S FUNCTION IN STORY

Shisui is one of the few people who sees Itachi as:

**Itachi.**

Not:
clan heir,
spy,
prodigy,
weapon.

This means if player begins becoming dangerously isolated:

Shisui should notice.

He can say:
you are making decisions for everyone.

The player can listen.

---

# 20. SHISUI CAN SURVIVE

Absolutely.

If player:
- accompanies him to Danzō;
- exposes Root;
- stops poison/ambush;
- uses force;
- convinces him not to sacrifice himself;

Shisui can live.

Then the entire coup story changes.

No invisible canon death.

---

# 21. IZUMI UCHIHA

Use **novel-expanded continuity** by default.

**Role:** childhood peer / emotional normal-life possibility / clan perspective / potential age-appropriate romance as they grow

Izumi:
- is Uchiha through her mother;
- lost her father in Nine-Tails attack in Shinden background;
- awakens Sharingan through grief;
- befriends Itachi;
- cares about him;
- becomes a shinobi.

She should not exist merely to die beautifully.

---

# 22. IZUMI’S VALUE

Izumi represents:

**the life Itachi never seriously allows himself to have.**

Friendship.

Tea.

Embarrassment.

Someone his age worrying he is carrying too much.

That should be playable.

If player prioritizes her:
relationship may become romance.

If not:
friendship.

If player is cold:
distance.

---

# 23. IZUMI CAN LIVE

If massacre never occurs:
obviously.

If massacre occurs:
player may spare her.

If player warns her:
she may flee.

If she survives:
she becomes a major person with her own view of what Itachi did.

Do not assume she forgives.

---

# 24. THE NINE-TAILS ATTACK

During Itachi’s early childhood.

Important consequences:

- Sasuke is an infant;
- Konoha is devastated;
- Uchiha are suspected because Sharingan can control Kurama;
- clan is restricted/relocated/monitored;
- mistrust deepens.

The player should experience:
danger first,
politics second.

---

# 25. NINE-TAILS NIGHT

Possible:
- protect Mikoto/Sasuke;
- help civilians;
- try to reach Police Force;
- ask why Uchiha are being held away from central fighting.

This plants the first clan-village fracture.

---

# 26. THE UCHIHA RELOCATION

After the attack, the clan’s physical/social separation matters.

Show:
- longer walk;
- adults whispering;
- surveillance;
- resentment.

Do not instantly explain coup.

Children grow up around this atmosphere.

---

# 27. ACADEMY — AGE SIX/SEVEN

The Academy phase should be more than one montage.

Itachi is:
- absurdly gifted;
- isolated by competence;
- bored in class;
- watched by adults.

The story should let player decide:

what does genius feel like?

Pride?
Loneliness?
Utility?

---

# 28. ACADEMY CHOICE ENGINE

Player can:
- deliberately hold back;
- help classmates;
- show off;
- become social;
- isolate.

These choices change:
- peer relationships;
- teacher perception;
- clan pride.

Do not force “stoic genius.”

---

# 29. ITACHI’S TALENT

By default:
- shurikenjutsu exceptional;
- ninjutsu learning extremely fast;
- tactical intelligence unusual;
- chakra control strong;
- physical skill advanced.

Do not use visible stats.

Show through action.

---

# 30. SHURIKEN TRAINING

This is a recurring visual motif.

Itachi’s ricochet/trajectory skill can feel incredible.

But preserve method.

Player may invent other specialties.

No need to railroad every fight into Sharingan.

---

# 31. GRADUATION

Itachi graduates extremely young.

This should feel:
- triumphant;
- unsettling.

Adults applaud.

The player is seven.

That contrast matters.

Fugaku sees opportunity.

Mikoto sees pride and fear.

Izumi sees him leaving childhood faster.

---

# 32. DANZŌ’S EARLY INTEREST

Shinden includes Danzō testing Itachi’s utilitarian thinking with moral questions.

Use the principle:

Danzō notices early that Itachi thinks at the scale of:
many lives vs one.

He begins cultivating him.

Do not make Danzō announce:
“I am evil and will manipulate you.”

He praises maturity.

That is more dangerous.

---

# 33. GENIN TEAM

Expanded continuity:

- **Tenma Izumo**
- **Shinko Inari**
- **Yūki Minazuki** / Minazuki-sensei depending naming continuity.

These characters should exist long enough to matter.

Do not rush them.

---

# 34. TENMA

Competitive.

Proud.

Initially irritated by Itachi.

Can become genuine teammate.

His death in expanded continuity is what helps awaken Itachi’s Sharingan.

If player changes event:
Tenma can live.

Then Sharingan may awaken another way later.

---

# 35. SHINKO

More socially grounded.

Eventually leaves shinobi life in Shinden after trauma.

This is important thematically:

**leaving is allowed.**

Itachi should see someone choose:
not to remain shinobi.

Maybe that plants another path.

---

# 36. TEAM MISSIONS

Use:
- missing animal;
- escort;
- reconnaissance;
- actual danger.

The player needs ordinary missions before politics consumes life.

This gives:
friendship,
competence,
normal childhood.

---

# 37. THE MASKED MAN ENCOUNTER

Expanded continuity links Tenma’s death to the masked man.

This is a strong early mystery.

Player sees:
a strange Uchiha-like ocular ability / impossible phasing.

They may:
- attack;
- flee;
- remember.

Do not reveal Obito identity.

Information state:
**masked Uchiha-like threat.**

---

# 38. SHARINGAN AWAKENING

Default:
traumatic teammate loss / helplessness.

But if Tenma survives:
the Sharingan should awaken later through another intense emotional event.

It is not a fixed cutscene.

Once awakened:
prose should change.

Itachi notices:
- movement;
- chakra;
- micro-actions.

Do not constantly describe red eyes.

---

# 39. CHŪNIN EXAMS

Shinden chronology has Itachi eventually allowed to take exam alone.

This is a strong “prodigy” phase.

Use it to show:
- pressure;
- admiration;
- political interest.

Player can:
- dominate;
- hold back;
- fail intentionally;
- refuse.

If they fail:
career changes.

No canon correction.

---

# 40. ITACHI AS CHŪNIN

Now adults give him real responsibility.

At age ten.

That should remain psychologically visible.

The story should not magically make him 25 because his rank is high.

He can be composed and still be ten.

---

# 41. FUGAKU’S ANBU PLAN

Fugaku realizes ANBU access can give Uchiha a direct channel into Konoha leadership.

His stated rationale may include:
representation.

The clan increasingly expects Itachi to become:
their eyes inside government.

This is the start of double life.

---

# 42. DANZŌ’S ANBU PLAN

Danzō wants:
an Uchiha he believes prioritizes village stability above clan.

Same boy.

Opposite handlers.

This is the dramatic machine.

---

# 43. MUKAI KOHINATA

From Shinden.

**Role:** traitorous Leaf shinobi / assassination test before ANBU  
**Style:** Gentle Fist lineage through distant Hyūga ancestry  
**Importance:** shows Danzō can make Itachi kill in the name of security before the massacre.

Mukai should not be disposable evil target.

He:
- sold secrets;
- has reasons;
- cares about family.

The player may:
- kill;
- capture;
- expose;
- refuse.

---

# 44. MUKAI CHOICE

Danzō frames mission as:
necessary elimination.

Player can ask:

> Why not trial?

If player refuses assassination:
ANBU entry may be blocked.

That is valid.

Maybe Hiruzen intervenes.

Maybe clan gets angry.

---

# 45. SHISUI ON MUKAI MISSION

Default:
Itachi chooses Shisui.

This strengthens bond.

They see:
how lethal Konoha’s secret work becomes.

This should become relevant later when Shisui considers using Kotoamatsukami.

---

# 46. ANBU ENTRY

Itachi enters ANBU very young.

He receives:
- mask;
- code identity;
- new authority;
- secret missions.

The art shift should feel iconic.

But not instantly make him emotionally blank.

---

# 47. KAKASHI HATAKE

Expanded ANBU continuity places Itachi around Kakashi.

**Role:** older prodigy / leader / mirror

Kakashi is useful because he is another person shaped by:
- childhood war;
- extraordinary talent;
- loss;
- ANBU.

Itachi can see a possible future in him.

---

# 48. KAKASHI’S RELATIONSHIP

Kakashi should be competent and relatively humane.

Itachi may expect “Friend-Killer” reputation and discover a person.

They can:
- respect;
- distrust;
- confide.

If player tells Kakashi about coup early:
this can create massive divergence.

---

# 49. ROOT VS ANBU

The distinction matters.

ANBU answers to Hokage.

Root answers to Danzō in secrecy.

Player should gradually understand:
Konoha is not one unified actor.

This is crucial.

If the story says “the village ordered the massacre” too simplistically, it loses nuance.

Hiruzen and Danzō differ.

The elders differ.

ANBU and Root differ.

---

# 50. HIRUZEN SARUTOBI

**Role:** Third Hokage / political authority / adult who wants peace but delays decisive intervention

Do not write Hiruzen as:
pure saint
or
secret mastermind.

His tragedy:
he wants negotiation.

He is too slow.

He allows Danzō too much operating space.

---

# 51. HIRUZEN’S POSSIBLE PATH

If player comes to him earlier with:
- evidence;
- trust;
- Shisui;
- Fugaku concessions;

he may act.

This is one of the major AU doors.

Possible:
- formal negotiation;
- expose surveillance;
- remove Danzō;
- give Uchiha representation.

Not guaranteed.

Clan radicals may reject.

---

# 52. THE UCHIHA COUP

Do not treat it as:
“the evil clan randomly wants power.”

The clan’s grievances include:
- suspicion after Nine-Tails;
- relocation;
- surveillance;
- political marginalization;
- frustration over Police role.

The coup is still dangerous.

Both facts coexist.

---

# 53. FUGAKU’S COUP GOAL

In Shinden-expanded continuity, Fugaku wants a bloodless takeover if possible.

That does not make it safe.

Plans involving:
- controlling leadership;
- possibly exploiting Naruto/Kurama in some adaptations

can escalate disastrously.

The player should be able to argue strategy.

---

# 54. CLAN RADICALS

Important names can include:
- Yashiro Uchiha;
- Inabi Uchiha;
- Tekka Uchiha.

They should have:
distinct motivations.

Not one mob voice.

Some want dignity.

Some want revenge.

Some want political change.

Some push Fugaku.

---

# 55. ITACHI AS DOUBLE AGENT

This is the best phase for Plotbreak.

Every conversation has two meanings.

Clan asks:
what did Hokage say?

ANBU asks:
what is clan planning?

Sasuke asks:
why are you never home?

Mikoto asks:
are you eating?

Shisui asks:
how long can this continue?

This is richer than combat.

---

# 56. INFORMATION STATE

Track separately who knows:

- clan coup details;
- Itachi’s ANBU reports;
- Danzō’s pressure;
- Shisui’s plan;
- masked man;
- surveillance;
- player loyalties.

This prevents everyone magically knowing the conspiracy.

---

# 57. PLAYER LOYALTY IS NOT A SINGLE METER

Itachi can:
- love Uchiha;
- oppose coup;
- distrust Konoha;
- love Sasuke;
all at once.

Do not reduce to:

VILLAGE 70 / CLAN 30.

Store positions semantically.

---

# 58. SHISUI’S KOTOAMATSUKAMI PLAN

Shisui proposes using his Mangekyō ability to influence Fugaku and derail coup.

This is morally complicated.

It may avoid bloodshed.

It also removes another person’s agency.

The player can:
- support;
- oppose;
- suggest target other than Fugaku;
- expose plan;
- use eye later differently.

This should be a major choice.

---

# 59. DANZŌ AND SHISUI

Danzō fears Shisui’s eye/power and believes his plan will not solve underlying rebellion.

He takes Shisui’s right eye in canon.

In Shinden, Root agents/poison create the ambush.

This is one of the biggest moments the player can potentially prevent.

---

# 60. SHISUI’S DEATH

If canon path:

Shisui entrusts remaining eye and mission to Itachi, then dies.

Itachi awakens Mangekyō.

This should be devastating.

No rushing into:
“NEW ABILITY UNLOCKED.”

Let grief exist.

---

# 61. MANGEKYŌ SHARINGAN

Once awakened:

abilities eventually include:
- Tsukuyomi;
- Amaterasu;
- Susanoo.

Do not frontload all instantly if source chronology/experience suggests mastery develops.

Prose should emphasize:
power has cost.

Eye strain.

Health.

---

# 62. PLAYER CAN SAVE SHISUI AND STILL AWAKEN MANGEKYŌ LATER

Yes.

No forced death.

The eye can awaken through another profound trauma.

Or perhaps never.

Then Itachi’s power ceiling changes.

Story adapts.

---

# 63. ITACHI’S BREAK WITH THE CLAN

After Shisui’s disappearance/death, clan suspects Itachi.

This can escalate:
- accusation;
- confrontation;
- Itachi lashing out.

Player may:
- stay calm;
- confess;
- lie;
- attack;
- expose Danzō.

No mandatory iconic outburst.

---

# 64. SASUKE WITNESSES TENSION

This is important.

Canon Sasuke sees pieces without understanding.

Player can:
- send him away;
- tell him;
- include him.

The player’s choices determine how betrayed Sasuke later feels.

---

# 65. DANZŌ’S ULTIMATUM

Canon pressure:

If coup happens:
war could kill clan, Sasuke, village.

Alternative Danzō offers:
Itachi helps annihilate Uchiha first; Sasuke spared.

This is the story’s central nightmare.

Do not present Danzō’s forecast as objectively certain.

It is his strategic prediction.

He may be right that war is likely.

He is not omniscient.

---

# 66. THE PLAYER MUST HAVE MORE THAN TWO OPTIONS

This is where interactive adaptation becomes meaningful.

Possible:

1. accept Danzō’s plan;
2. tell Fugaku;
3. tell Hiruzen;

After massacre, Itachi eventually stops believing Konoha deserves his loyalty, tells Kisame the truth and genuinely joins Akatsuki’s political project.

### Player 8 — Itachi Lives
He reaches the final fight, sees what years of manipulation have done to Sasuke, abandons the death plan and asks his brother to help him destroy the masked man instead.

### Player 9 — Hokage
The coup is solved without massacre. Itachi stays. Years later the prodigy who once believed true shinobi worked only from shadows has to lead openly as Hokage.

### Player 10 — Ordinary
He refuses accelerated promotion while still young, spends more time with Sasuke and Izumi, and becomes an excellent but not mythologized shinobi. Maybe the most radical Itachi story is one where adults never convince a child he has to save the world alone.

All ten are unmistakably **ITACHI**.

Because the heart of the story is not:

> **the Uchiha massacre must happen.**

It is:

> **a boy capable of carrying impossible burdens is surrounded by adults who keep handing him more—and this time, the player decides when he finally puts one down.**
